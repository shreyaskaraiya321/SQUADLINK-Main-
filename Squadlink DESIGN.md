## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, tier-one esports aesthetic that rejects the juvenile cliches of flashing RGB bars, oversaturated rainbow text, and harsh neon cyan-magenta gradients. Drawing directly from the polished chrome, pale champagne gold, and royal violet luster of the provided insignia, the visual language balances elite competitive prestige with the restraint of luxury engineering.

The brand persona is authoritative, surgical, and premium—catering to professional esports athletes, high-stakes tournament organizers, elite rosters, and discerning gaming enthusiasts. Every interface element operates with weighted intention: matte dark obsidian backdrops, micro-hairline borders of brushed metallic platinum, and decisive, single-source accents in warm champagne gold and deep amethyst violet. The overall visual style is **Tactile Minimalism infused with Metallic Glass and Tonal Obsidian Layering**, creating a mature, bespoke atmosphere where competitive data and team identity take center stage without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high dynamic range depth within low-luminance spaces.

- **Primary (`#E5D8B8`) — Champagne Gold:** Used for high-priority CTA fills, primary active states, tournament trophy badges, and key metric emphasis. Provides exceptional contrast on dark backdrops without the glare of harsh white. Supported by `#F3EBDD` for peak highlights and `#C2B28B` for interactive hover shifts.
- **Secondary (`#7B52AB`) — Amethyst Violet:** The competitive signature color. Applied to focus states, team rank indicators, secondary actionable items, and selected navigation segments. Supported by `#9D71D0` for subtle glowing indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#4A2E75`) — Deep Regal Violet:** Utilized for sunken structural frames, subtle surface highlights, active tab backplates, and container boundaries.
- **Neutral Palette (`#0D0B12`, `#14111D`, `#1C1728`):** The foundation is obsidian deep slate. Base canvases sit on `#0D0B12`, surface containers elevate to `#14111D`, and interactive cards sit at `#1C1728`. Muted text utilizes `#8F879D`, while high-contrast label typography leverages `#EDE8F5`.
- **Constraint Rule:** Multi-colored linear rainbow fills and chaotic animated gradient borders are strictly prohibited. Gradients are limited exclusively to subtle monochromatic depth fades (e.g., `#E5D8B8` fading softly to `#C2B28B`, or `#14111D` fading to `#0D0B12`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the technical, geometric sharpness of **Space Grotesk** for brand anchor points and data headlines with the clean, ultra-readable neutral precision of **Hanken Grotesk** for player stats, tournament schedules, chat feeds, and body narratives.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in `Space Grotesk` with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to project an intentional, tournament-grade precision. 
- **Labels & Overlines:** Set in `Space Grotesk` in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), establishing military/tactical squad telemetry readability.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts:** Set in `Hanken Grotesk` with standard letter tracking and generous proportional line heights for instant readability across chaotic match environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined **12-column fluid grid system** anchored to maximum desktop bounds of `1440px` with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 48px (`3rem`) page margins.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns. High-density tournament brackets, live match streams, and squad roster drawers sit side-by-side without overlap.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Secondary panels collapse into sliding drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Single-stream vertical stacking with sticky bottom navigation.

Spacing internally adheres to an 8px modular baseline scale. Component padding scales rigorously from `space-xs` (4px micro-tags) to `space-xl` (40px hero section breaks).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via **Tonal Obsidian Layering and Micro-Hairline Edge Lighting** rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Floor):** Deep slate canvas `#0D0B12`. Completely flat, non-reflective.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Sub-cards):** Obsidian surface `#14111D` with a 1px solid hairline border of `rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.08)` (subtle gold ambient rim).
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Match Fixtures):** Elevated violet-tinted slate `#1C1728`, bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(123, 82, 171, 0.25)` and a localized diffused ambient shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(7, 5, 10, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Squad Bars):** Elevated surface `#211C30` backed by a 16px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), a 1px hairline border of `rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.22)`, and a deep, directional shadow: `0px 20px 48px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0px 0px 1px rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.4)`.

No fuzzy rainbow multi-color glows are used. Glows are strictly restricted to focused states in mono-source violet (`0 0 12px rgba(123, 82, 171, 0.4)`) or gold (`0 0 12px rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft, Precision-Machined Corner Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, dropdown trigger targets, badges) utilize a base radius of `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- Mid-sized elements (data tables, match scoreboards, modal dialogues) utilize `rounded-lg` (`8px` / `0.5rem`).
- Large surface wrappers and display cards use `rounded-xl` (`12px` / `0.75rem`).

This restrained corner radius communicates the precision of gaming hardware and avoids the casual, toy-like appearance of fully pill-shaped or bubble geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Champagne Metallic Solid):** Solid background of `#E5D8B8` with dark obsidian typography (`#0D0B12`, `fontWeight: 600`). On hover, transitions cleanly to `#F3EBDD` with a subtle elevation shift. No gradients.
- **Secondary (Regal Violet Outline):** Ghost surface with 1px border of `#7B52AB`, text in `#EDE8F5`. On hover, fills with `rgba(123, 82, 171, 0.16)` and border deepens to `#9D71D0`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text in `#8F879D`, on hover shifts to `#EDE8F5` with background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (Live, In-Match, Verified):** Height 24px, padding 0 8px, border-radius 4px. Features a solid 6px indicator pip in primary gold or secondary purple. Background is dark slate tint (`rgba(20, 17, 29, 0.8)`) with a 1px border of `rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.15)`. Text is set in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Cards & Match Brackets
- **Surface:** `#14111D` with a 1px perimeter border of `rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.08)`.
- **Header:** Features a dedicated 48px baseline height with integrated gold or violet team metadata markers.
- **Hover State:** Card hairline border intensifies to `rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.25)` with a 2px vertical lift. No multi-colored glow pulses.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#0D0B12`, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, font `Hanken Grotesk 14px`, text color `#EDE8F5`, placeholder `#5F586C`. Focus state shifts border directly to `#E5D8B8` with zero outer ring bloom.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px square (or circle) with 1px stroke of `rgba(229, 216, 184, 0.3)`. Checked state fills with solid `#E5D8B8` displaying a sharp `#0D0B12` checkmark icon.

### Rosters & Stat Lists
- **Rows:** Alternating rows leverage `#14111D` and `#110F18`. Bottom 1px hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`. Hovering a squad row highlights with `rgba(123, 82, 171, 0.08)` and activates an inset 2px left vertical border in `#E5D8B8`.
